# omniKPI workspace

Direction: professional enterprise SaaS for consolidating division reports and reviewing KPI performance. Avoid bento layouts, decorative grids, competition branding, and visual hype.

Brand direction updated from the owner's LOGO.png: deep navy and saturated blue. Modern analytics-console references include Wazuh, CloudWatch and Google Analytics; use their task-focused hierarchy as inspiration, without copying their layouts. Navy application chrome anchors identity. Blue identifies actions and selection. Cool white or navy surfaces keep dense data readable. Red, amber, and green are reserved for performance state. Segoe UI provides readable compact labels without a remote font dependency. Flat summaries and ruled tables prioritize comparison over decorative cards. The original logo is displayed unchanged in onboarding.

ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The performance table is the focal point; uploads and evidence review support that workflow. Light and dark themes share semantic tokens and retain the user's selection locally.
