# omniKPI and SDG 9

## The goal

SDG 9 is “Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization and foster innovation.” ITASE's guidebook permits teams to select Goal 9. For this telco-focused product, explain a specific connection to digital infrastructure rather than claiming broad impact across all of Goal 9.

## Strongest connection for this project

**Primary alignment: Target 9.1, resilient infrastructure.** Target 9.1 calls for quality, reliable, sustainable and resilient infrastructure that supports economic development and human well-being. A telecom network is digital infrastructure. omniKPI's reporting workflow can help performance teams bring divisional KPI submissions into a shared, reviewable view, identify readings missing their targets, and preserve source context for follow-up. That is a plausible operational support pathway, not proof that omniKPI itself makes a network more resilient.

**Telecom context: Target 9.c, access to ICT and the Internet.** Target 9.c concerns increasing access to information and communications technology and pursuing universal, affordable Internet access in least developed countries. Reliable telco operations are relevant to this goal, but omniKPI does not measure population coverage, affordability, adoption or the official indicator 9.c.1. Mention 9.c as sector context, not as an outcome measured by the product.

Official target wording and indicator definitions: [UN Sustainable Development Goal 9](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9). The UN identifies indicator 9.c.1 as the proportion of population covered by a mobile network, by technology.

## A defensible impact pathway

`Division reports -> reviewed shared KPI view -> clearer visibility for the responsible team -> follow-up and operational decisions -> potential support for network reliability`

Only the early data workflow and the prototype's follow-up interface are demonstrated in the current app. Faster visibility, better operational decisions, improved network availability, expanded coverage and social impact are hypotheses that require deployment and measurement.

For the deck, make three distinctions explicit:

1. **Current output:** validated KPI readings, target status and source attribution in one prototype workspace.
2. **Intended operational contribution:** give network performance teams a clearer basis for reviewing and following up on divisional reports.
3. **Unmeasured SDG impact:** population access, affordability, mobile-network coverage and improved service reliability are not measured by this demo.

## How to show the connection without overclaiming

- Use a small relationship diagram: divisional reporting -> analyst visibility -> network follow-up -> potential infrastructure reliability support.
- Tie examples to service-related indicators only as workflow examples. Label demo values as synthetic.
- State that SDG 9 is the societal direction, while the prototype evaluates a reporting process. Do not label a KPI dashboard as an SDG measurement system.
- Avoid claiming direct contribution to 9.2 (industrial employment/GDP), 9.4 (resource efficiency and cleaner industry), or 9.5 (research capacity) unless the deck gives a specific, measured connection.

## Relationship to the submitted proposal

The submitted proposal discusses targets 9.1, 9.2, 9.4 and 9.5, and proposes six facility-level indicators related to SDG 9. The current telco prototype does not implement an SDG indicator dashboard or measure those outcomes. The final deck can narrow the framing to Target 9.1, with 9.c as telecom context, and explain the indirect pathway honestly. This narrows the presentation to the product's current scope; it does not claim that the proposal's broader indicators are implemented.

## Suggested spoken version

“We align omniKPI with SDG 9 because telecom networks are part of the digital infrastructure people and services rely on. Our prototype focuses on the operational reporting layer: it brings divisional KPI submissions into a reviewed view with their sources attached. That can support teams responsible for infrastructure reliability, which is an indirect contribution to Target 9.1. We do not measure population coverage or affordability under Target 9.c, and our synthetic demo does not prove improved network outcomes.”
