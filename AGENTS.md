# Repository guidance

For business or innovation case decks, read and apply `skills/business-innovation-case/SKILL.md` for action titles, evidence, and the storyboard format. For product evaluation, user journeys, UX clarity, or pitch storytelling, read and apply `skills/product-thinking-strategic-narrative/SKILL.md`. Apply both when a deck needs product reasoning and a strategic narrative. These skills supplement the project-specific guidance below; explicit user requirements and competition constraints take precedence over their defaults.

When creating, revising or reviewing a competition deck, pitch, or important project presentation in this repository, read and apply `.agents/skills/omnikpi-deck-style/SKILL.md`. Use `Deck_Example/STYLE_NOTES.md` for the user's reference-derived visual and storytelling preferences.

For an ITASE 7.0 deck, also follow `Deck_Context/ITASE_DECK_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `Deck_Context/SDG_9_POSITION.md` and `Deck_Context/CURRENT_SYSTEM.md`. Current code and demo fixtures define what is implemented; the submitted proposal is historical context and may include planned features. Keep synthetic data, proposed impact and measured results clearly distinct.
