# RESEARCH & CONTENT BRIEF — OMNIKPI TELCO

Role: data research, business analysis and final presentation preparation.
Current objective: support OmniKPI's ITASE 7.0 finalist presentation.

## Product story

Network teams work with fragmented reports and operational logs. OmniKPI brings
district KPI readings into one matrix, highlights statistical deviations, shows
historical context and uses retrieved incident evidence to draft an advisory RCA.
The Harvester maps uploaded report headers and returns a preview for review.

The project was inspired by telco dashboard experience during an internship.
All demo data is synthetic; no internal company data is reused.

## Claims discipline

No training from scratch: the system uses an existing model through a router.
The heatmap is deterministic and serves stored seed statistics. RCA narration may
still be wrong; retrieval and deterministic confidence do not eliminate hallucination.
Confidence is heuristic, not an accuracy result. Recommendations are not executed.
Harvester does not yet import confirmed data. Financial and response-time benefits
are projections unless measured. Saved evidence includes failure examples worth
discussing honestly in Q&A.
